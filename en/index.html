Poker Room CHONMAGE

TEXAS HOLD'EM IN FURUMACHI, NIIGATA

POKER ROOM CHONMAGE is a bright, relaxed poker room in Furumachi, in the center of Niigata City. Beginners and solo visitors are welcome, and you are free to come in just to look around first.

Amusement poker: no cash betting

CHONMAGE is an amusement poker room. There is no betting with money and nothing can be exchanged for cash. You pay a facility fee and play for the game itself.

Prices for other tournaments and ring games vary by day. Ask the staff, or check our X account (posts are in Japanese).

New to poker?

  1. Look aroundFeel free to see the room before deciding whether to play.
  2. Free beginner lessonStaff explain the hand rankings and rules of Texas Hold'em from the start.
  3. Join a freerollWith the facility fee from ¥500, you can enter a freeroll tournament at no extra cost.

The room has three tables in an open, white space, and staff are always there. You don't need to bring anything except money for the fee.

What's on today

We run freerolls, tournaments and ring games. Each day's schedule is posted on the homepage and on our X account, both in Japanese. Changes on the day are also announced on X.

If you can't make a tournament's registration in time, you can call ahead to have a seat held (called 置きバケ, okibake). The call deadline differs from the tournament's final registration time, so please check on X or with the shop.
